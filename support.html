Getting started

  1. Pick a heading style in the Calculator tab. Pinch pleat is the default; Ripplefold, Euro, goblet, box, pencil, grommet, rod pocket, tab top, flat panel and wave are a tap away.
  2. Enter the rod or track width and the finished length. The keypad has fraction keys, so 54½ is two taps.
  3. Choose the fabric width, pattern repeat and lining. The fabric to order, cuts and widths update with every key.
  4. Open “How it was figured” under the results to see each step.
  5. Save the window to a project to total fabric across windows, print a work order or send a quote.

How the fabric is figured

Each panel finishes at its share of the rod plus its return and overlap. That is multiplied by the fullness, both side hems are added, and the total is divided by the usable fabric width and rounded up to a half or whole width. The cut length is the finished length plus the heading and hem allowances, rounded up to whole pattern repeats when there is a repeat. Fabric to order is the cuts times the cut length, rounded up to the step chosen in Settings. In a project, fabric is added up across the windows that share a cloth before it is rounded.

Ripplefold

Snap counts, snap tape and stackback follow the stack guides in the Kirsch Ripplefold Fabrication Manual for Architrac, Estate and Hollis tracks at 60, 80, 100 and 120 percent fullness, with butt or overlap master carriers. Other makers' tracks can differ by a snap; check their chart if you are not hanging on Kirsch.

Workroom standards

Settings → Workroom Standards holds the allowances every new window starts with: fabric width, heading, hem, side hems, seams, return, overlap, half widths and track system. Windows you have already saved keep their own numbers, so old quotes do not change.

iCloud sync

Projects and workroom standards sync through your own private iCloud between iPhone and iPad. There is no Pleatwise account. If a project is changed on two devices, the later change is kept. Switch sync off in Settings if you prefer to keep everything on one device; a backup file can move your work to any phone.

Reminders

Pleatwise sends at most two kinds of notification, both optional and both set from a project: one the evening before an installation date and one when a quote has gone unanswered for the number of days you choose. Turn them off in the project or in iOS Settings.
