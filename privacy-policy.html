Your work.
Your devices.

This policy explains how Pleatwise, developed by Enes Furkan Bayram, handles information in its iPhone and iPad app and on this website. Contact: enfurbay@gmail.com.

What Pleatwise collects

Nothing. Pleatwise has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive your projects, measurements, client names, prices or settings. Calculations run on your device.

Where your data lives

Projects, windows, workroom standards and business details are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is on (it is on by default when you are signed in to iCloud), the same data is also stored in your private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and only your Apple Account can read. Turning sync off in Settings stops further syncing; data already in iCloud can be removed from iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Documents you create

Work orders, quotes, spreadsheets and backup files are created only when you ask for them and go only to the destination you choose in the share sheet. They can contain client names and addresses you entered, so review them before sharing. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

Notifications

Notification permission is requested only when you switch on a reminder in a project. Reminders are scheduled on the device and contain the project name and window count. You can turn them off in the project or in iOS Settings.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, ratings and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under Apple's own privacy practices. Pleatwise never sees your payment details.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for support. Ask us to delete support correspondence at any time.

This website

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Pleatwise is a professional tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If these practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
