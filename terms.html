What the app is for

Pleatwise is a calculating aid for drapery workrooms. It turns the measurements and allowances you enter into fabric quantities, cut sizes, pleat layouts, snap counts, quotes and work orders. Its results depend entirely on what you enter and on rules of thumb that vary between workrooms.

Your responsibility

Check every measurement, allowance, fabric width, pattern repeat and total before you order or cut fabric, and before you send a quote. You are responsible for the work you produce and the prices you quote. Ripplefold figures follow the published Kirsch stack guides; other makers' hardware can differ.
